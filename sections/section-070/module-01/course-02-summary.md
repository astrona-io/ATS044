# Summary

Escalation analysis examines how initial permissions can reach other identities, resources or host authority. RBAC scope and additive grants contribute to the picture, alongside workload creation, mounts and diagnostic interfaces.

Authorization, admission, runtime and network controls restrict different stages. Protect authority over those controls and inspect durable consequences after compromise. Least privilege narrows impact while artifact and application trust remain necessary.
