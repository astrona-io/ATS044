# Privilege Escalation And Indirect Authority

This module develops the reasoning needed to distinguish direct grants from routes to broader authority.

## Learning Goals

- Distinguish direct grants from routes to broader authority.
- Identify risky workload creation, identity and host interfaces.
- Relate RBAC, admission and runtime restrictions to escalation prevention.
- Explain why effective permission review includes alternate paths.

## Before You Start

Read the examples as descriptions of system behavior. No cluster, account, laboratory or installed tool is required. Technical terms are defined where they are used.
