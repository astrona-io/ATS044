# Privilege Escalation

This section teaches privilege escalation and indirect authority. These ideas help you explain system behavior and choose a suitable approach in a KCSA scenario.

You will work through Privilege Escalation And Indirect Authority and Preventing Escalation With Controls. Each module has a landing page, deep-dive parts with hands-on steps in a playground, a graded lab and a summary. The section ends with a capstone lab that combines the skills of both modules and a knowledge check.

## Learning Outcomes

- Distinguish direct grants from routes to broader authority.
- Identify risky workload creation, identity and host interfaces.
- Relate RBAC, admission and runtime restrictions to escalation prevention.
- Explain why effective permission review includes alternate paths.
