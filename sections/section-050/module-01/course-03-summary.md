# Summary

Network threats include unwanted observation, alteration and peer impersonation. Connection restriction and transport security protect different stages, and certificate verification establishes the intended peer for the protected hop.

Trace discovery, endpoints, forwarding and application responses separately. A permitted or encrypted connection does not grant every business operation, and protection ends where plaintext or another unverified hop begins.
